## Global rules for this section
- Caregiver-facing surfaces: 24-hour Western-digit times ("18:00", shipped formatHm), NO gold anywhere, NO counts/badges on the caregiver's row or door, no gamification.
- Status is always icon + Arabic label; numerics LTR-isolated (dir="ltr").
- Sheets: FigmaBottomSheet chrome, 19px title, grab handle, no nested confirms.
- Frames marked "after the rebuild" (11b photo preview/replace/remove) are for the photo-capture rebuild only — ship the "ships now" variant today.
- Dashed-border wells inside frames are ANNOTATIONS to the implementer (OS alerts, flagged proposals) — never render them.
